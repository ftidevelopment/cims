How It Works

An employee can be assigned as Kaizen Approval Authority for multiple departments.

Example:

  • Engineering → Budi
  • Maintenance → Budi
  • Production → Andi

In this example, Budi can approve or reject Kaizen from Engineering and Maintenance, but not Production.
